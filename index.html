Meet the widget

This floating assistant is a single chat-widget.js + chat-widget.css pair any site can embed. It matches questions against a built-in knowledge base with weighted keyword and phrase scoring, handles greetings and small talk, asks for clarification when two topics are close, and remembers context for follow-ups like “what about Saturday?”.

  • No frameworks, no network calls, works offline
  • Configurable name, colors, and knowledge base
  • Typing indicator, quick-reply chips, answer feedback

Try asking…

  • “What are your hours?”
  • “Do you take Delta Dental?”
  • “How much is whitening?”
  • “My tooth hurts, what do I do?”
